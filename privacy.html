Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Lastpost is an iPhone app with a Safari extension that hides Reels, Explore, suggested posts and ads on instagram.com. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect anything.

What we collect

Nothing. Lastpost has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app and the extension never send your data to us.

What the Safari extension sees

When you allow Lastpost on instagram.com, the extension reads the page in Safari so it can hide the parts you've chosen. This happens entirely on your iPhone. Lastpost never sees your Instagram password, never logs in on your behalf, and never copies, stores or sends your posts, messages or contacts anywhere.

Your stats

The numbers in the app are measured on your iPhone: how long you spent on instagram.com in Safari each day, and how many reels, suggestions and ads were kept out of your feed. Time is counted only while instagram.com is on screen in Safari and you're using it; nothing about what you see or do on the page is recorded. To count each post only once a day, the extension keeps a short list of post codes for the current day; it's cleared the next day.

iCloud

If you use iCloud, your Lastpost settings and these daily numbers are also saved in your own iCloud account, so a new iPhone or a reinstall picks them up. This copy belongs to your Apple Account and is stored by Apple; we can't see it. Deleting the app removes the data from your iPhone.

Filter updates

To keep working when Instagram changes its website, the extension may download an updated list of filter rules from our website (bydlapp.github.io) about twice a day. This file contains only rules, and the request includes no information about you. Like any website, GitHub, which hosts the file, may see your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Lastpost Pro is sold through the App Store. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Children

Lastpost doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Lastpost is made by Galip Alp Demirel. Questions? Email support@bydl.app.

Lastpost is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
