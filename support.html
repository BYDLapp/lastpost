Setting up

  1. Open Lastpost and tap Open Instagram in Safari.
  2. In Safari, tap the page menu on the left of the address bar, choose Manage Extensions and turn on Lastpost.
  3. Open the menu again, tap Lastpost and choose Always Allow on This Website.
  4. Sign in to Instagram in Safari. Safari remembers you.

Common questions

Does it work in the Instagram app?
No. Apple doesn't let apps change other apps, so Lastpost works on instagram.com in Safari. We suggest moving the Instagram app off your home screen and putting Lastpost there instead.

Reels are showing again.
Instagram sometimes changes its website. Lastpost updates its filters automatically; if something slips through for more than a day, email us and tell us what you see.

Lastpost says "Setup needed".
Open instagram.com in Safari once with the extension allowed. The app notices as soon as the extension runs.

How do I cancel Lastpost Pro?
Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Lastpost. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

I bought Pro on another iPhone.
In Lastpost, go to Settings → Restore purchases.
